## 2. Product Search

### TC-03: Search for a product
- Open the main page.
- Enter a product name in the search field.
- Click the Search button.
- Expected result: Relevant products are displayed.

### TC-04: Search with an empty field
- Open the main page.
- Leave the search field empty.
- Click the Search button.
- Expected result: The system does not display search results for an empty query.

## 3. Shopping Cart

### TC-05: Add a product to the cart
- Open a product page.
- Click the Add to Cart button.
- Open the shopping cart.
